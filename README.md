# 🧭 Kechf AI

> Uncovering hidden career paths and simulating personalized job fits for Algerian youth, beyond social pressure.

## 📌 The Problem
In Algeria, youth (ages 17–25) frequently select university majors based on family expectations and social popularity rather than personal alignment. This leaves over 80% of students completely unaware of true, specialized career prospects (e.g., discovering that a Computer Science background opens doors to IT Auditing).

## 🚀 The Solution: Kechf
**Kechf** is an AI-driven orientation platform designed to break societal molds. It provides:
1. **Hidden Career Mapping:** Exposes cross-disciplinary roles across sectors like Finance, Medicine, and Computer Science.
2. **Psychological & Goal-Based Simulation:** Leverages psychological frameworks (RIASEC / Big Five) combined with real market data.
3. **Interactive Job Simulation:** Simulates real-world daily scenarios to match a student's unique personality with the career where they will genuinely thrive.

## 🛠️ Tech & Methodology
* **Core Engine:** Large Language Models (LLMs) configured with specialized system prompts for psychological profiling and career matching.
* **Frameworks:** RIASEC Model & Big Five Personality traits integration.
* **Target Audience:** Students and young professionals (17–25) in Algeria and emerging markets.
